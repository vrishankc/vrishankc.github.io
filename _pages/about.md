Hi! My name is Vrishank, and I'm a rising sophomore at MIT majoring in Computer Science, with minors in Mathematics and Biology. I'm passionate about ML research, specifically surrounding multimodal representation learning, foundation models, and multi-agent reinforcement learning. Currently, I conduct research at the Vázquez-García Lab across the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, Harvard Medical School, and Massachusetts General Hospital, and was recently at the Aghaeepour Lab in Stanford's Department of Biomedical Data Science.

Previously, I was at Stanford in the Department of Pediatric Cardiology, investigating how variably penetrant mutations can cause rare cardiovascular diseases.

Outside of research and engineering, I love to play tennis, try new recipes, and [sing](https://www.instagram.com/vrosings).

Feel free to contact me at [vrishank@mit.edu](mailto:vrishank@mit.edu)! I'd love to chat.

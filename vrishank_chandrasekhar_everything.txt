Cholesterol and gasdermin collaboration (Broad Institute and Ragon Institute)
- Led the computational analysis testing whether an interaction between cholesterol biosynthesis and gasdermin E (GSDME) predicts response to cancer therapy.
- 18 public cohorts, about 4,000 patients: The Cancer Genome Atlas (6 cohorts, about 3,000 samples, survival), 8 immunotherapy cohorts (about 1,000 samples, response and survival), 4 neoadjuvant chemotherapy cohorts in breast cancer (pathologic complete response).

GRACE: Graph-Reinforced Assessment of Cardiac Events with Multimodal Data
MIT 6.3930 final project, team of two, September 2026 to present
github.com/vrishankc/grace
--------------------------------------------------
- Predicts acute myocardial infarction, acute heart failure, and atrial fibrillation in the emergency department from MIMIC-IV multimodal data.
- Two prediction times: 6 hours and 24 hours after arrival. Every input must be timestamped at or before the prediction time.
- Cohort: 425,087 emergency department stays, filtered to 164,629 stays from 91,175 patients at 6 hours and 177,803 stays from 96,628 patients at 24 hours.
- Inclusion: adults, valid arrival and departure times, an available label, at least one ECG or troponin/BNP result in the window.
- Labels from ICD-9 and ICD-10 codes of the linked hospitalization, or emergency department codes when not admitted. Acute presentations only. Atrial flutter kept as a separate label. A broader heart failure label kept for sensitivity analysis.
- Labels validated against MIMIC-IV-ECG-Ext-ICD: 100% agreement for acute heart failure and atrial fibrillation.
- Prevalence at 6 hours: acute myocardial infarction 3.4%, acute heart failure 7.1%, atrial fibrillation 14.5%.
- Splits: 70/15/15 train, validation, and test from a salted SHA-256 hash of the patient ID (114,913 / 24,886 / 24,830 stays at 6 hours). All of a patient's visits stay in one split.
- Hyperparameters tuned on an inner 15% patient holdout inside the training set. Test scored once after models are frozen.
- Chunked processing of multi-gigabyte tables, caching, and a shared training harness.
- Baselines: XGBoost and TF-IDF with logistic regression on vitals, labs, ECG measurements, and radiology text.
- Team's fused multimodal models: 0.93 to 0.96 validation AUROC for acute myocardial infarction, with patient-clustered bootstrap confidence intervals.
- Language model evaluation pipeline comparing MedGemma and Gemma 3 (in progress): zero-shot Yes/No probability scoring, KV-cache reuse, frozen-embedding classifiers, paired AUROC tests, knowledge-graph augmentation ablations.
- Metrics: AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score, subgroup analysis.
